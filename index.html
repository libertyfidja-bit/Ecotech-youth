Business Model
How EcoTech Youth sustains itself
A multi-stream revenue model built for long-term self-sustainability — not dependent on donations alone.
Value Proposition
🌱
For Youth
Free access to green job listings, skill-building courses, and project funding — lowering every barrier to entry into the green economy.
🏢
For Employers
Access a pipeline of job-ready, trained green-economy talent. Post jobs, host training, and build employer brand with youth audiences.
🤝
For Partners
NGOs, government and universities get a trusted platform to reach youth, deploy programs, and measure real-world environmental impact.
Revenue Streams
Growth Roadmap
PHASE 1 · Months 1–6
Launch & Validate
Launch platform with 50 green jobs. Onboard 300 youth users. Partner with 3 NGOs. Run 2 pilot training cohorts. Validate job placement model.
PHASE 2 · Months 7–18
Scale & Monetise
Reach 1,000 users across 5 districts. Launch employer subscription. Publish first community project reports. Seek government endorsement.
PHASE 3 · Year 2+
Regional Expansion
Expand to Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania. License platform to universities. Launch EcoTech Youth Fellowship for top project founders.
