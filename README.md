# 🌿 EcoTech Youth

> **Rwanda's platform connecting young people to green economy jobs, skills and community projects.**

## 📌 About the Project

**EcoTech Youth** is a full-stack web platform built for the **MKU × University of Westminster × ACE Creative Economy Hackathon (November 2024)**. It addresses youth unemployment, environmental sustainability, and mental wellbeing by connecting Rwandan youth to purposeful, income-generating green work.

The platform was designed and built by a **Computer Science student at Mount Kigali University (MKU)**, combining technical skills with a deep understanding of Rwanda's green economy landscape.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Rwanda has thousands of talented, ambitious young people. Yet:

- 🔍 Green economy jobs exist — but youth **cannot find** them
- 📚 Skills exist — but there is **no clear pathway** to eco-employers
- 💡 Community eco-ideas exist — but **lack funding and support**
- 📱 No single platform connects **tech + environment + youth employment**

---

## 💡 The Solution

EcoTech Youth operates across **three core functions**:

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **Learn** | Free micro-courses on green tech, eco-business & environmental skills |
| 💼 **Connect** | Green job board linking youth to employers in clean energy, agri & media |
| 🤝 **Build** | Community project marketplace — pitch, fund, and mentor eco-solutions |

---

EcoTech Youth addresses **all 6 Creative Economy Hackathon themes**:

| Theme | How EcoTech Youth Delivers |
|---|---|
| 🎨 **Creativity & Entrepreneurship** | Youth turn creative & tech skills into real green enterprises |
| 💼 **Job Creation & Economic Growth** | Direct green economy job placement — target 200 jobs in Year 1 |
| 🧠 **Mental Health & Wellbeing** | Purposeful work builds confidence, community and income |
| 🤝 **Community Impact** | Local eco-projects funded and mentored through the platform |
| 🏛️ **Culture & Heritage** | Stories told through a Rwandan lens with local language support |
| 🌍 **Environmental Sustainability** | The platform's core mission — every job, course & project must show environmental benefit |

---

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Green Jobs Board** — Filter by category (clean energy, agri, media, education)
- ✅ **Learning Hub** — 6 courses with progress tracking and certificates
- ✅ **Community Projects** — Funded project listings with support and pitch tools
- ✅ **Join Platform Modal** — Youth registration flow
- ✅ **Mobile Responsive** — Works on all screen sizes
- ✅ **Dark Mode Support** — Respects system preference

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** — Single-file platform, no frameworks needed
- **CSS3** — Custom design system with CSS variables and dark mode
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Event-driven navigation and dynamic rendering
- **Google Fonts** — Space Grotesk + Lora typefaces
- **Vercel** — Static hosting and deployment
