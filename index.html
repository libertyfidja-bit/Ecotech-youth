Business Model
How EcoTech Youth sustains itself
A multi-stream revenue model built for long-term self-sustainability — not dependent on donations alone.
Value Proposition
🌱
For Youth
Free access to green job listings, skill-building courses, and project funding — lowering every barrier to entry into the green economy.
🏢
For Employers
Access a pipeline of job-ready, trained green-economy talent. Post jobs, host training, and build employer brand with youth audiences.
🤝
For Partners
NGOs, government and universities get a trusted platform to reach youth, deploy programs, and measure real-world environmental impact.
Revenue Streams

Growth Roadmap
PHASE 1 · Months 1–6
Launch & Validate
  • Launch with 50+ green jobs listed
  • Onboard 300 youth users across Kigali
  • Partner with 3 NGOs for content & listings
  • Run 2 pilot paid training cohorts
  • Target: 30 verified job placements
PHASE 2 · Months 7–18
Scale & Monetise
  • Reach 1,000 users across 5 districts
  • Launch employer subscription model
  • Secure government endorsement from REMA
  • Hire 2 district community coordinators
  • Launch mobile app (Android-first)
PHASE 3 · Year 2+
Regional Expansion
  • Expand to Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania
  • License platform to 3+ African universities
  • Launch EcoTech Youth Fellowship
  • Target: 10,000 users across East Africa
  • Raise Series A from impact investors
