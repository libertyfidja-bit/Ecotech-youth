Business Model

How EcoTech Youth Sustains Itself

A multi-stream revenue model built for long-term self-sustainability — not dependent on donations alone.

Value Proposition

Who does EcoTech Youth serve?

🌱
For Youth (Free)

Free access to 8+ green job listings, 12 learning courses, community project funding, and project submission tools — lowering every barrier to entering the green economy.

🏢
For Employers (Paid)

Access a pipeline of job-ready, trained green-economy talent. Post jobs, host training programs and build employer brand with Rwanda's most motivated youth audience.

🤝
For Partners (Contracted)

NGOs, government agencies and universities get a trusted platform to reach youth, deploy employment programs and measure real-world environmental impact at scale.

Growth Roadmap

3 phases to East African scale

PHASE 1 · Months 1–6

Launch & Validate

  • Launch with 50+ green jobs listed across Rwanda
  • Onboard 300 youth users across Kigali
  • Partner with 3 NGOs for content and listings
  • Run 2 pilot paid training cohorts (20 students each)
  • Validate job placement model — target: 30 placements
  • Apply for MINECOFIN youth enterprise grant
PHASE 2 · Months 7–18

Scale & Monetise

  • Reach 1,000 registered users across 5 districts
  • Launch employer subscription model — 10 paying employers
  • Secure government endorsement from REMA or RDB
  • Hire 2 district community coordinators
  • Launch Android mobile app
  • Publish first annual impact report
PHASE 3 · Year 2+

Regional Expansion

  • Expand to Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania
  • License platform to 3+ African universities
  • Launch EcoTech Youth Fellowship program
  • Target: 10,000 users across East Africa
  • Raise Series A from impact investors
  • Launch multi-language support
